Gradient norms concentrated near their maximum

100% of the last 50 grad_norm readings sit within 0.5% of their observed maximum (1). Constant gradients and post-clip logging can both produce this pattern. The norm history alone cannot establish whether clipping was used, its threshold, or how often it changed updates.
